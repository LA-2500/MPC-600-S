MPC 600 S
Quick Start: Installing

What You Need

  • An Akai MPC Sample.
  • An SD card. Any size; formatted FAT32 or exFAT, as computers and the MPC Sample format them.
  • A computer to copy one folder onto the card.
  • mpc-os.zip, the MPC 600 S, from the download page.

1. Put It on the Card

  1. Unzip mpc-os.zip. Inside is one folder, autorun.
  2. Copy the autorun folder to the top level of the SD card: not inside another folder, and with its name and the files in it unchanged. The card should look like this: 
    SD card
└── autorun
    ├── autorun.check
    ├── autorun.json
    ├── autorun.sh
    └── mpc-os
  3. Eject the card from the computer properly before taking it out.

2. Start It

  1. With the MPC Sample off, put the card in.
  2. Turn the MPC Sample on. It starts as usual, and a few seconds later the MPC 600 S takes over the screen and shows its title screen, then its main page.

The MPC 600 S runs from the card every time it is in. Nothing is installed on the MPC Sample itself.

3. Your Work

The first time it runs, the MPC 600 S makes a folder named MPC600S at the top of the card. Your projects, programs, samples, settings, effects chains and exports go there (see the manual’s chapter on saving and loading). Keep the card in while you work.

4. Back to the Stock MPC Sample

Turn the MPC Sample off and take the card out (or remove the autorun folder from it). It starts as it came from Akai; nothing on it was changed.

5. Updating

Download the new mpc-os.zip and replace the autorun folder on the card with the new one. The MPC600S folder, with your work and settings, is not touched.

Optional: Stems

The Stems page (SAMPLE REC pressed until it shows Stems) separates a pad’s sound into its drums, bass, vocals or music. It needs three model files that are too big to go with the OS, so they are a separate download: MPC600S-stems.zip, about 107 MB. Everything else works without them; the Stems page says No stem models on the card until they are there.

  1. Download MPC600S-stems.zip and unzip it on the computer. It holds a folder MPC600S, with a folder stems in it, with the three files.
  2. Copy them to the card so that they end up in MPC600S/stems/ at the top of the card, next to autorun: 
    SD card
├── autorun
└── MPC600S
    └── stems
        ├── umxhq-bass.bin
        ├── umxhq-drums.bin
        └── umxhq-vocals.bin
  3. If the card has no MPC600S folder yet (the MPC 600 S has not run from it), copy the whole MPC600S folder from the zip to the top of the card.
  4. If the card already has an MPC600S folder, with your projects and settings in it, copy only the stems folder into it. Do not replace the MPC600S folder: on a Mac, Finder’s Replace would delete your work in it. On Windows, copying the folder over the old one and choosing to merge is also fine.
  5. Keep the file names as they are, and eject the card properly.

The models stay on the card: updating the OS (the autorun folder) does not touch them. A sound of up to 20 seconds can be separated; it takes about 5 seconds for each stem of a 15-second sound. To remove them, delete the stems folder.

If It Does Not Start

  • Check the autorun folder is at the top of the card, not in another folder (unzipping sometimes makes an extra folder around it), and that nothing in it was renamed or edited: the MPC Sample checks the files and will not run them otherwise.
  • Give it time: the stock app starts first, and the MPC 600 S takes over when it is up, up to 45 seconds after power-on.
  • Try another card, or format this one in a computer (FAT32 or exFAT) and copy the folder again.
  • The MPC 600 S keeps a log, autorun/mpc-os.log, on the card: it says what it found at start-up.

Getting Around

Press SHIFT + SAMPLE REC on any page for its help. The full manual, MPC-600-S-Manual.pdf, covers everything.
